Orbit Clock Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

Orbit Clock is an alarm clock, timer and stopwatch app. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

What we collect

Nothing. Orbit Clock does not collect, store on any server, sell or share any personal data. The app has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no internet access. Android doesn't give it network permission.

What stays on your phone

Your alarms (times, repeat days, labels, chosen sound, snooze length), timer and stopwatch state are saved only on your device, so they're still there after you close the app or restart your phone. Uninstalling the app deletes them. Android's own backup may include this app data if you have device backup turned on. That backup is handled by Google under your Google account settings, not by us.

Permissions and why

  • Alarms & reminders / exact alarms: to ring at exactly the time you set.
  • Notifications and full-screen notifications: to show the ringing alarm, including over the lock screen.
  • Run at startup: to set your alarms again after the phone restarts.
  • Foreground service / wake lock / vibration: to keep the alarm sound playing and vibrating until you snooze or dismiss it.

The sound picker only reads the name of the alarm sound you choose so it can play it.

Children

The app does not collect any data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
